Why Steady

Six decisions that shape how the app treats your routines, your data, and your bad days.

Privacy you can verify

Most habit apps treat your daily routines, health habits, and mental struggles as data points to monetize or feed to advertising brokers. Steady stores 100% of your data locally in an SQLite database on your device.

  • No account registration required
  • No analytics trackers — no Firebase, no Mixpanel
  • The release build doesn't even request external internet permissions
Gentle streaks — a safety net for real life

Real life doesn't happen on an unbroken chain. Getting sick, travelling, or taking a mental health break shouldn't wipe out months of dedication.

  • Streak freezes keep your momentum alive without breaking your historical continuity
  • Retroactive recovery — forgot to log yesterday? Apply a freeze within your recovery window
All habit types, one calm place
  • Simple checks — daily or scheduled habits with a single satisfying tap and subtle haptic feedback
  • Count habits — track daily numeric goals: glasses of water, pages read, pushups, minutes meditated
  • Medication & dose slots — multi-slot daily reminders (morning, noon, night), each dose marked on its own slot
Health Connect auto-sync

Connect Android Health Connect to fulfil daily goals automatically:

  • Step tracking — auto-completes walking and step goals from your device step counter
  • Hydration sync — logs water intake seamlessly
  • Sleep duration — syncs sleep time to verify bedtime routines

Every sync stays strictly on your phone — never uploaded to the cloud.

Daily notes with honest, deterministic insights

Write honest reflections alongside your daily habit checks. A built-in, local tone and vocabulary engine notices when you're struggling, celebrates when you rebound, and offers encouraging nudges — without a creepy cloud AI reading your journal.

True data portability & AES-256 backups

You are never locked in.

  • Export anytime — one-click CSV and JSON export to keep your data in spreadsheets or personal archives
  • Encrypted backups — AES-256-GCM backup envelopes, protected by your personal hex key token

Questions people actually ask

Do I need an account to use Steady?

No. Steady has no account signup, email verification, or passwords. Open the app and start building habits immediately.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. Steady stores your habits, history, notes, and preferences in an SQLite database on your local device storage.

What happens if I change phones?

Export your data as a JSON file, or create an AES-256 encrypted backup and restore it on your new device with your private key.

Does Steady sell my health data or show ads?

Never. Steady contains zero advertising SDKs and zero third-party analytics trackers, and doesn't sell or share any user data.

Is Steady a medical application?

No. Steady is a personal routine and habit planner. While it offers medication reminder slots and hydration tracking, it doesn't provide clinical diagnoses, medical advice, or treatment plans.
