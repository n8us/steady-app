Is Steady Ember free?

Yes — with one clearly marked boundary between what is free and what is not.

Updated 27 September 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer

Yes. The free tier supports up to 6 habits at once, and streaks, notes and history stay fully intact on every one of them. Unlimited habits are part of the paid tier. Neither tier carries ads, an account requirement or a trial timer.

What the free tier includes

The free tier is the whole app, capped at one dimension: how many habits are active at the same time. Six at a time, with nothing switched off behind a paywall.

  • Up to 6 habits active at once
  • Streaks, streak freezes and the retroactive recovery window
  • Daily notes with the local insight engine
  • Full history, statistics and heatmaps
  • CSV and JSON export, plus AES-256-GCM encrypted backups
  • Health Connect sync for steps, hydration and sleep

There is no countdown attached to any of it. The free tier does not become a paid tier after 7 days, and no feature quietly stops working when a trial period ends, because there is no trial period.

Archiving is how you free a slot

When a habit has finished its job — a course completed, a treatment ended, a season passed — archive it. The history stays exactly where it is, the active slot frees up, and you can start something new in its place. Archiving pauses a habit rather than deleting it, so the record remains part of your stats.

Do archived habits still count towards the six?

No. The limit applies to habits on your active dashboard. Archived habits keep their full streak and history but do not occupy a slot, which is why finishing one habit is usually enough to begin the next.

What the paid tier adds

One thing: unlimited habits. Streaks, freezes, notes, history, export, encryption and Health Connect behave identically on both tiers. The current price is shown in the app listing on Google Play rather than repeated on this page, because it can vary by region and by platform.

What neither tier includes

  • Advertising of any kind, in any placement
  • An account, an email address or a password
  • Analytics SDKs — no Firebase, no Mixpanel, no tracking of your routines
  • A trial timer counting down towards a paywall

Steady Ember is free to use as a habit tracker within the 6-habit limit, and it stays that way for as long as you keep it installed. Six is enough for most people's daily set; when it stops being enough, the paid tier removes the ceiling and nothing else changes.
